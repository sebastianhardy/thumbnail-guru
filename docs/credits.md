## Research underpinning this tool

The 20-pattern hook library is distilled from Sebastian's viral-text-hooks research (unpublished), which draws on:

- Loewenstein (1994) — curiosity-gap theory
- Kahneman & Tversky (1979) — loss aversion
- Cialdini — influence principles (authority, commitment, scarcity)
- Zeigarnik — interrupted-action memory effect
- Ogilvy and modern Meta ad research — specificity ≻ generality

Plus pattern analysis across AI / agency / creator Reels and TikToks from creators including Rowan Cheung, Matt Wolfe, Nick Saraev, Alex Finn, Iman Gadzhi, Charlie Morgan, Codie Sanchez, Greg Isenberg, Sahil Bloom, Justin Welsh, Brett Williams, Ben Tossell.

None of those creators endorse this tool. They are research references only.
