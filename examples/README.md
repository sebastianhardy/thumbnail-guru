# Examples

Drop your own reference thumbnails in this folder for inspiration.

During onboarding (`tf onboard`), you'll be asked to provide up to 10 reference thumbnails from your niche. You can paste URLs or local paths at that stage. This folder is just a convenient place to keep local references if you prefer having them in-repo.

**Note:** reference thumbnails you use during onboarding are copied to `~/.thumbnail-forge/references/` — not stored in this repo. So anything you drop here is for your own reference only and won't affect generation.

## Tips for good reference thumbnails

- Pick thumbnails from **your** niche, not just viral ones
- Aim for 10 references so patterns emerge
- Include both channels you admire AND channels you want to beat
- Mix styles — Thumbnail Forge generates multiple style variants, so your references can span minimal, chunky, and 3D-extruded

Good luck.
